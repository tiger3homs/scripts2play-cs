### **🚀 CS User Config v3 - Released! 🚀**

Hey everyone!

I'm excited to announce a major update to the **CS User Config** script, completely refactored for a much better experience. This script automatically runs your custom commands every time you join a server.

---

### ✨ **What's New in v3?**

*   **Modern UI**: Say goodbye to the old menu! There's now a sleek, floating **gear icon** (⚙️) that opens a clean modal window for all your settings.
*   **Better Controls**: The new UI makes it easier than ever to add, edit, enable/disable, and delete your commands.
*   **Hideable Icon**: If the gear icon is in your way, just press `Alt + Shift + X` to hide it. Press the keys again to bring it back.
*   **Stable & Fast**: The entire script has been rewritten from the ground up for better performance and reliability.

---

### **How to Get It**

You can find the updated script and full instructions in the repository here:
[https://github.com/tiger3homs/scripts2play-cs/tree/main/UserConfig2](https://github.com/tiger3homs/scripts2play-cs/tree/main/UserConfig2)

To install, just grab the code from `v3.user.js` and paste it into a new Tampermonkey script.
